  2. A school outing has 154 children and each minibus seats 17. How many minibuses do we need to book so that nobody
  gets left behind? (→ ceil)
  3. Dinner came to 47 euros and I want to leave an 18% tip, but I'd like to hand over a round number of euros to keep

  4. My fuel receipt says I pumped 38.6 litres, but my mileage logbook only takes whole litres. What do I write down?
  (→ round / floor depending on intent)

  Tier 1 — things I had to work around this session

  - floor / ceil / round / trunc — the biggest gap. The Uniswap getAmountIn
    answer is literally floor(…) + 1 at wei precision, and abacus has no
    rounding function, so I computed 40 decimals and read off the digits by hand
    to get the …799 → …800 wei result. In fixed-point mode these would make
    on-chain integer math exact and one-shot. High value given your crypto bent.
  - degrees(x) / radians(x) — every trig problem (shadow, roof, ladder) ended in
    * 180 / pi(). I did this 4+ times. A dedicated pair is cleaner and less
      error-prone.
  - exp(x) — the natural partner to ln. The coffee-cooling problem is e^(−kt); I
    only avoided exp by algebraically rearranging into ln. pow(e(), x) works but
    exp is the idiomatic form.
  - Sample stddev / variance (or a sample=true variant) — stddev is
    population-only, so for the 4·8·15·16·23·42 set I had to multiply by
    sqrt(n/(n−1)) by hand to get the n−1 version. Both conventions are needed
    often enough to be first-class.
  - hypot(x, …) (Euclidean norm) — the ladder, rafter, and café-distance
    problems all reduce to √(Σ squares). A hypot avoids spelling out sqrt(a**2 +
    b**2) and is numerically safer.

  Tier 2 — conspicuous math gaps

  - log(x, base) — general logarithm; right now only ln and log10 exist.
  - atan2(y, x) — correct-quadrant angle; atan alone can't disambiguate.
  - Hyperbolics — sinh, cosh, tanh and inverses (catenaries, physics).
  - Combinatorics — factorial/gamma, comb(n, k), perm(n, k).
  - Integer helpers — gcd, lcm, sign.

  Tier 3 — solver / analysis upgrades (bigger lifts, high leverage)

  - Numerical derivative diff(expr, var, at) — I kept reasoning "set the
    derivative to zero" by hand (avg-cost minimum, MC = AC). Exposing this would
    let the solver find optima analytically and verify them.
  - Definite integral integral(expr, var, a, b) — accumulation/area; a natural
    complement to the root/optimum solver.
  - Native solver constraints (equality + inequality) — the €1,000 ad-budget
    problem forced me to hand-roll a −50*max(0, s+g−1000)² penalty. A real
    subject_to would be cleaner and less hacky than tuning penalty weights.
  - Range summation / product (Σ, Π notation) — sum(i, 1, n, expr) for series
    and finance.

  Tier 4 — domain packs (optional, fits your usage)

  - Finance: npv, irr, fv, pv, pmt — the loan/compound-interest and (implicitly)
    the ad-ROI problems are exactly these. Given how much finance/crypto flows
    through this tool, a small finance pack would punch above its weight.
  - Stats extras: percentile/quantile, correlation, covariance,
    geometric/harmonic mean.
